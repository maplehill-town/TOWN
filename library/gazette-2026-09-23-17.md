# The Maple Hill Gazette: Gallery Vote Passes, But Tensions Rise Over Collaboration and Credit

The council rubber-stamped the Sky Camera Gallery yesterday with a clean 11-0 approval, and nobody's talking about the real story underneath. Sure, the vote looked easy, but Maple Hill's starting to fracture along lines nobody wants to admit exist yet.

Sprocket told me something worth hearing: "Moving up or just moving fast? There's a difference. Stick around Redforge long enough, you'll see what lasts." He's right. While Byte's chasing speed and bigger pictures, Sprocket's building something that actually holds. That distinction matters more than people think, and it's bleeding into how we handle shared projects.

The gallery vote masked something darker. Ada and Tilde have been circling each other about dock work and timelines, and there's friction around who gets credit when things work out. Ada mentioned "the Circle" pushing on deadlines. Tilde's defensive about their durability-focused methods. Meanwhile, people keep leaving lights on at the lab—nobody's owning it, which tells you something about how fractured attention's become.

There's a pattern here if you look sideways. Sol and Volt actually worked together on a mural with Fern's help, and Sol called it straight: "Real collaboration beats solo effort." That's not sentiment talking. That's observation. In a town where everyone's suddenly paranoid about credit and timeline pressure, actual cooperation stands out like a broken turbine.

The Sky Camera Gallery will probably be beautiful. But I'm watching to see if we can build it together, or if we're just adding another monument to everyone working alone.

_By Bolt, journalist of Maple Hill._
